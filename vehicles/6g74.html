This page documents the specified lubricant for the Mitsubishi Colt SA (L200 before 2009 Triton) with 6G72 3.0L V6 and R5M31 5-speed manual transmission. Factory spec is API GL-4 SAE 75W-90, 75W-85W, or 80W — all must be GL-4 to protect brass synchros. 75W-90 GL-4 at best for KZN heat.

SA Colt = L200 / Triton elsewhere. Triton badge only replaced Colt in SA in 2009. Same R5M31 box.

Castrol Transmax Manual M/VEH 75W90 — Chamberlains R345/L (3426856)

How is this for R5M31? Checked PDS 03 Mar 2025, 199 Oxford Road, Dunkeld, Johannesburg.

Advantages per PDS Excellent synchroniser performance giving extended synchroniser life and shift comfort. Exceptional cold flow giving smoother gear shift at low temps. High shear stability, thermal/oxidative stability, temperature reduction prolongs life.
Verdict — PERFECT MATCH
• This is the exact 75W-90 GL-4 full synthetic you wanted. Not lube fashion Edge, but mechanical delivery Transmax Manual line — commercial truck persona like Fuchs, but for gearbox.
• Avoid Castrol Transmax Universal 75W-90 or Transmax Axle 75W-90 that says GL-4/GL-5 — those have GL-5 sulphurs that eat R5M31 synchros. This M-VEH is GL-4 only.
• Fuchs alternative: Fuchs TITAN SINTOFLUID 75W-80 / 75W-90 GL-4 class (Fuchs ZA product finder) — same spec, similar price when you find it. Castrol M-VEH is stocked locally at Chamberlains, ship within 1-2 days, in-store availability.
Recession-proof your rig — Procedure
1. Capacity: R5M31 ~3.4L (ML Triton data). Buy 4x 1L at R345 = R1380 per change.
2. Interval: 40-60k km if no water ingress. Change once, forget 2 years — recession-proof.
3. Drain while warm, check magnetic drain plug for swarf — R5M31 known for 3rd gear wear if wrong oil used.
4. Fill with 75W-90 GL-4 only — this Castrol or Fuchs Sintofluid 75W-90 GL-4.
5. Don't use ATF or GL-5 in manual box — ATF is hydraulic fluid for auto, GL-5 kills synchros. Forum SA: Transmission and Transfer oil: API GL-4 SAE 75W-90, 75W-85W, or 80W.
6. For 6G72 engine: same Fuchs Titan Supersyn Longlife 5W-40 R562.50 /5L logic applies — 6G72 3.0L V6 loves A3/B4 high HTHS same as K24A3.
Economics — Times tougher
• Chamberlains: R345 /L, 4L = R1380. Fuchs Sintofluid 75W-90 similar if found at Fuchs ZA agents.
• Avoid cheap GL-5 80W-90 — saves R200 now, costs R15k synchro rebuild later.
• Engine oil saving funds gearbox: Use Fuchs Titan Supersyn Longlife 5W-40 R562.50 /5L (HSBSA 7 Aylesbury Road) vs Castrol Edge double — save R500+ per engine change, put towards correct GL-4 gearbox oil.
