LAB #001 K24A3 CL9 TYPE S NO CL7 PARTS

HONDA ACCORD EURO CL9 (K24A3 6MT) - Hose Fitment Note

MOTORLabs — Richards Bay, KZN — MotorLove with heart

This page documents a possible fitment issue where a hose with CL7 geometry is sometimes supplied as compatible with CL9. On the K24A3 6-speed Manual chassis, the CL7 hose does not follow the correct routing.

Stock OEM spec is the reference. Verify against OEM catalog before install. No aftermarket cooling upgrades. OEM thermostat 78C.

Thermostat Housing Ruggedizing — K24A3

The K24A3 uses a plastic thermostat housing that becomes brittle with heat cycles. Ruggedizing means replacing the plastic housing with an aluminium housing while retaining OEM thermostat 78°C. No low-temp 68°C thermostat — ECU must reach closed-loop.

What to source:
• Aluminium thermostat housing — K24A3 / K24A2 compatible, includes O-ring groove
• OEM thermostat 78°C — Honda 19301-PNA-003 or equivalent
• OEM O-ring and gasket — verify against OEM catalog
• Torque: housing bolts 12 N·m, bleed cooling system at heater core
Where to find parts — same method as aluminium radiator sourcing:
1. Check OEM part number in Honda EPC (Electronic Parts Catalog) for CL9 K24A3 6MT
2. Search local Honda parts stockists in SA with that OEM number (Durban, Richards Bay, Gauteng)
3. For aluminium housing: search for K24 aluminium thermostat housing — verify fitment is K24A3 not K20, check that it retains OEM thermostat size (not integrated low-temp)
4. For aluminium radiator (previous discussion, not yet documented): same — search by OEM radiator core size for CL9 K24A3 manual (no ATF cooler), verify inlet/outlet routing matches K24A3, not CL7 geometry. Source from radiator specialists who build to OEM dimensions, not universal.
Stock reliability principle: aluminium housing + OEM 78°C thermostat + OEM radiator cap 1.1 bar = ruggedized, not souped up. No low-temp thermostat, no aftermarket fan switches.
