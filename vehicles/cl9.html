This page documents a possible fitment issue where a hose with CL7 geometry is sometimes supplied as compatible with CL9. On the K24A3 6-speed Manual chassis, the CL7 hose does not follow the correct routing.

Stock OEM spec is the reference. Verify against OEM catalog before install. No aftermarket cooling upgrades. OEM thermostat 78C.

Thermostat Housing Ruggedizing — K24A3

The K24A3 uses a plastic thermostat housing that becomes brittle with heat cycles. Ruggedizing means replacing the plastic housing with an aluminium housing while retaining OEM thermostat 78°C. No low-temp 68°C thermostat — ECU must reach closed-loop.

What to source:
• Aluminium thermostat housing — K24A3 / K24A2 compatible, includes O-ring groove
• OEM thermostat 78°C — Honda 19301-PNA-003 or equivalent
• OEM O-ring and gasket — verify against OEM catalog
• Torque: housing bolts 12 N·m, bleed cooling system at heater core
Where to find parts — same method as aluminium radiator sourcing:
1. Check OEM part number in Honda EPC (Electronic Parts Catalog) for CL9 K24A3 6MT
2. Search local Honda parts stockists in SA with that OEM number (Durban, Richards Bay, Gauteng)
3. For aluminium housing: search for K24 aluminium thermostat housing — verify fitment is K24A3 not K20, check that it retains OEM thermostat size (not integrated low-temp)
4. For aluminium radiator (previous discussion, not yet documented): same — search by OEM radiator core size for CL9 K24A3 manual (no ATF cooler), verify inlet/outlet routing matches K24A3, not CL7 geometry. Source from radiator specialists who build to OEM dimensions, not universal.
Stock reliability principle: aluminium housing + OEM 78°C thermostat + OEM radiator cap 1.1 bar = ruggedized, not souped up. No low-temp thermostat, no aftermarket fan switches.

Castrol Edge vs Fuchs Titan Supersyn Longlife 5W-40 — Technical Delivery vs Bang for Buck

K24A3 Fit Perfect — A3/B4 high HTHS keeps pressure at 7000rpm VTEC. Honda specifies 5W-30 A3/B4 or 5W-40 A3/B4. Fuchs 5W-40 exceeds. Perfect — same HTHS, same Honda spec. No advantage in engine.
Economics (SA 2026) R562.50 /5L = R112.50 /L. At 4.2L K24A3 sump + filter = R472 per change. 10k km interval = R0.047 /km. Recession-proof. ~R1100-1200 /5L = R220-240 /L. Same sump = R924-1008 per change. 10k km = R0.09-0.10 /km. Double price for same protection.

SA Colt Rig — R5M31 Manual + 6G72 3.0L V6 — Gearbox Oil

Your SA Colt (L200 before 2009) with 6G72 3.0L and R5M31 5-speed manual: factory spec is API GL-4 SAE 75W-90, 75W-85W, or 80W — all must be GL-4 to protect brass synchros. 75W-90 GL-4 is the at-best choice for KZN heat.

Castrol Transmax Manual M/VEH 75W90 — Chamberlains R345 /L (3426856) — How is it?
• API GL-4 only — not GL-4/GL-5 universal — correct for R5M31
• MB-Approval 235.72, Meets Ford WSD-M2C200-C, Suitable PSA B71 2330
• Full synthetic, Smooth Drive Technology™, Viscosity 15.09 mm²/s @100C, VI 226, Brookfield 20000 mPa.s @-40C, Pour -54C, Flash 190C
• Advantages per Castrol PDS: excellent synchroniser performance, extended synchro life, shift comfort, exceptional cold flow for smoother shift at low temps, high shear stability, thermal/oxidative stability, temperature reduction prolongs life
• 199 Oxford Road, Dunkeld, Johannesburg spec sheet — South African PDS dated 03 Mar 2025
Verdict for your Colt R5M31 + 6G72:
• PERFECT MATCH — this is the exact 75W-90 GL-4 full synthetic you wanted. Not the lube fashion Edge, but the mechanical delivery Transmax Manual line — commercial truck persona like Fuchs, but for gearbox.
• Avoid Castrol Transmax Universal 75W-90 or Transmax Axle 75W-90 that says GL-4/GL-5 — those have GL-5 sulphurs that eat R5M31 synchros. This M-VEH is GL-4 only.
• Capacity: R5M31 ~3.4L (ML Triton data). Buy 4x 1L at R345 = R1380 per change. Interval 40-60k km if no water ingress. Recession-proof: change once, forget 2 years.
• Contrast to engine oil debate: For K24A3 engine, Fuchs R562.50 /5L beats Castrol Edge double price. For R5M31 gearbox, this Castrol Transmax M-VEH R345 /L is actually good value for GL-4 full synthetic — Fuchs Sintofluid 75W-80/90 class is similar price when you find it, but this is stocked locally at Chamberlains (ship within 1-2 days, in-store availability).
Recession-proof your rig:
1. Drain while warm, check magnetic drain plug for swarf — R5M31 known for 3rd gear wear if wrong oil
2. Fill with 75W-90 GL-4 only (this Castrol or Fuchs Sintofluid 75W-90 GL-4) — 3.4L
3. Don't use ATF or GL-5 in manual box — ATF is hydraulic fluid for auto, GL-5 kills synchros
4. For 6G72 engine: same Fuchs Titan Supersyn Longlife 5W-40 R562.50 logic applies — 6G72 3.0L V6 loves A3/B4 high HTHS same as K24A3
