MotorLabs Procedure — 3 Belt System

  1. Take a photo of all 3 belt routings before removal.
  2. Alternator belt: Loosen alternator pivot and tensioner MD368209 (14mm). Remove belt. Spin MD368209 — must be silent, no lateral play greater than 0.5mm.
  3. AC belt: Loosen AC tensioner, remove. Spin its pulley.
  4. P/S belt: Loosen P/S pump and tensioner MD303884, remove. Spin pulley — check JTEKT Koyo PU207021RR1XY if replacing.
  5. Replace pulleys that are rough and fit 3 new belts. Torque tensioner bolt 44-48 Nm for MD368209, similar for MD303884.
  6. Tension: Alt belt 5-7mm deflection mid-span, AC 5mm, P/S 5-7mm. Do not over-tighten — it damages alternator bearing.
  7. Run for 2 minutes, re-check tension, and listen.
  8. If throttle body was cleaned in same job: disconnect battery for 10 minutes, then idle relearn with AC OFF then AC ON.

Torque and Specs

Guide / Tensioner pulley MD368209: 44-48 Nm | P/S Tensioner MD303884: 40-45 Nm | Belt deflection: 5-7mm | Alternator pivot: 45 Nm
